# Fleet-E2E — 104 real sites through the prospect pipeline (2026-07-23)

**What this is.** An end-to-end test of the paste-URL funnel's worker path
(`scripts/audit/granska-site.ts --mode=onboarding`) over 104 real SaaS websites
(`scripts/day0-sites.ts`) — the exact pipeline a prospect hits when they paste
their URL into `/try`: freeze the page → extract the content model → produce
deterministic findings → run a **gated** lift test on a frozen copy → capture
before/after screenshots → emit a self-contained report. Runner:
`scripts/fleet-e2e/run.ts` (concurrency 3; per-site freeze cached).

**Why it matters for customers.** The before/after example IS the funnel's
retention hook: *paste your URL → see your own page improved, honestly → then
Stripe*. Before this test, that hook fired **zero** times across the fleet.

|---|---:|---:|---|
| **before/after example shown** (`pass` + demo image) | **0** | **32** | a real, pixel-verified demo of the customer's own content |

**32 of the 101 renderable sites (≈32%) now produce an instant, honest,
pixel-verified before/after of their own homepage — with zero manual work.**
Baseline: 0.

Sales translation: *for every 100 SaaS homepages pasted into the funnel, ~32
prospects immediately see their own content rearranged and pixel-verified, ~11
get an honest "this one needs you in the loop" (itself a trust signal), ~12 get
an honest "we won't guess here", and ~10 more are reachable with one extraction
improvement.*

## What changed — three fixes, all preserving the honesty contract

The demo path reused the **serving loop's** acceptance bar ("clean pass only").
On a **frozen copy** the page's scripts are inert, so two checks routinely can't
run (the CTA hit-test and the LCP servability check) and the gate emits a
**warn** — *vacuity*, not damage. The baseline counted all 47 holds the same;
**34 were pure vacuity**, so **zero** before/after examples were produced.

1. **Accept vacuity-warns honestly** (`577030d`). `demoPass()` accepts a `warn`
   as demo-worthy **iff every reason is a vacuity warn** (`isVacuityWarn`, which
   lives beside the warn strings in `render-gates.ts` with unit coverage). The
   hard damage gates (overflow, above-hero, clickability, reversibility) still
   fail hard. The **serving loop is untouched** — production still demands a
   clean pass. Pass-text is honest under vacuity (drops the CTA clause when
   `ctaChecked===0`, scoped to "every layout check").

2. **Visual-delta guard** (`9d02f24`). A frozen copy with remote images blocked
   can render near-blank (calendly: 95% white), and an inner-scroll layout makes
   `fullPage` capture only the first screen while the lift happens below it
   (posthog: the gate measures a real 2041px rise, the screenshot misses it).
   Either way before/after come out visually identical. granska now pixel-diffs
   the pair (in-browser canvas, no new deps) and below 2% rows-changed keeps the
   pass but **suppresses the images** and says why — never two look-alike images
   under a claimed rearrangement. Withholds 3 of 35 (posthog/calendly/loops).

3. **Ambiguous-target guard** (`9d02f24`). `move_up` resolves its section via the
   first 24 chars of the heading (`measure.findByLocator`: substring, first DOM
   match). Two sections whose headings share those 24 chars but differ in full
   text (juni: "Business accounts built for global payments" vs "…for growth")
   let the gate lift the **wrong** section while the report describes another.
   granska now refuses when the needle matches more than one **distinct** heading
   — identical duplicate headings stay allowed (either lifts the same content).
   Moved juni and monday to honest refusals.

Plus two finding-copy fixes (`428a0d4`): the "No trust signals" finding carries
the JavaScript-render caveat the conversion finding already had; "sits far down"
only says so when the target is genuinely in the lower half.

## Honesty verification (the whole product rests on this)

Every pass was checked empirically, not by assertion:

- **0** passes claim "0 conversion element(s) still clickable".
- **0** shown before/after pairs are byte-identical; every shown `after`
  pixel-differs from its `before` by ≥2% of rows (guard-enforced).
- The 3 suppressed passes are the honest cases (blank/short frozen copy) — the
  lift is valid, but no misleading image is shown.
- The 11 holds are **all** honest structural refusals (no-prev-sibling, no-rise,
  above-main). Zero environmental vacuity remains.
- The 12 refusals are honest: 10 structural + 2 duplicate-heading (juni, monday).

**Demo strength of the 32 shown passes** (fraction of page rows that visibly
changed, pixel-diffed): **24 STRONG** (≥15%; railway 72%, neon 59%, customerio
58%, ghost 55%, wrike 52%), **7 MEDIUM** (5–15%), the rest just over the 2% floor.

## Where the remaining value sits (next levers, ranked)

1. **Heading-proof recall — the #1 lever (+~10 demo-worthy sites).** Of the 43
   `None` sites, **10 carry real proof in a section *heading*** that the detector
   misses because `containsTrustSignals` is computed from section *bodies* only
   (`proofFromBody(body)` in `extract.ts`), never headings — and there is no
   guarantee detector:
   - front — "9,300+ customers trust Front…"
   - typeform — "Join 150,000+ businesses driving revenue"
   - coda — "4 ways 50,000+ teams use Coda…"
   - activecampaign — "See results in 30 days or get your money back" (guarantee)
   - …plus airtable, asana, fortnox, moz, n8n, netlify

   Could push shown-passes from 32 toward ~42. **Caveat:** `containsTrustSignals`
   also feeds the production redesign engine's designer brief (the `[proof]`
   marker in `context.ts`) and can flip a section's *type* (`proof && generic →
   type=proof`), so it is **golden-contracted** (extract.test.ts + structure-eval
   + snapshot). This must ship as a CI-validated change — kept out of this pass
   deliberately.

2. **Freeze image fidelity (root cause of the 3 suppressed + some thin sites).**
   The static freeze inlines stylesheets but not remote images/webfonts, and
   never rewrites `srcset`/`sizes`, so image-heavy pages render blank in the
   demo. Inlining/​pinning image geometry during freeze would recover several
   suppressed demos. (Local browser-render is network-blocked here, so this is
   also why 3 sites `freeze_failed`.)

3. **Thin-site handling (33 genuinely-`None` sites).** Mostly SPA shells whose
   static freeze yields a near-empty DOM (aircall, algolia, amplitude, apollo,
   auth0, clerk, dropbox, ramp, render, resend, rippling, segment, zapier, …).
   The pipeline correctly declines to invent findings; the "No trust signals"
   finding now discloses the JS-render caveat too.

4. **Real value already surfaced beyond lifts:** 8 sites have genuine **mobile
   horizontal-scroll bugs** the demo flags honestly (semrush 986px, pandadoc
   890px, mailchimp 599px sideways on a 390px screen) — measured against the
   frozen copy's inlined CSS, so these are real, not artifacts.

## Method notes / reproducing

- `bun run scripts/fleet-e2e/run.ts --conc=3` (env
  `PLAYWRIGHT_CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE_PATH`). Resumable; writes
  `fleet-e2e/results.json` after every site; per-site freeze cached in
  `fleet-e2e/<name>/frozen.html`, so a re-run costs ~3s/site. `fynd.json` gains
  `flyttShown` (pass with a shown demo vs a valid pass with no image).
- Baseline preserved as `fleet-e2e/results-baseline.json`. All `fleet-e2e/`
  artifacts are gitignored (heavy + regenerable); the numbers live here.
- 3 `freeze_failed` sites (canva, gusto, personio) fail only because the local
  browser-render path is network-blocked in this container; they freeze fine on
  the Actions runner.

## Adversarial bug-hunt (7-dimension workflow, each finding verified)

A parallel bug-hunt audited the whole pipeline for correctness + honesty defects
and adversarially verified each finding. The three highest-severity honesty bugs
it confirmed are the three fixed above (no-op/​invisible before/after; wrong-section
lift via 24-char heading collision; vacuity accepted with misleading copy). The
rest are recorded below with their disposition.

**19 candidate findings, 18 verified → 13 CONFIRMED, 5 REFUTED** (26 agents,
each finding adversarially checked). Disposition:

### Confirmed and fixed in this pass

| Finding | Sev | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong-section lift: `move_up` resolves via the first 24 chars of the heading, so a heading collision can move a different section than the report describes | high | ambiguous-target guard (`9d02f24`) |
| A real DOM reorder the screenshot can't show (inner-scroll / blank freeze) is shown as a passing before/after | high (harm) | visual-delta guard (`9d02f24`) — see note below |
| "Sits far down" asserted for an above-the-fold index-2 section | low | conditional copy (`428a0d4`) |
| "No trust signals" finding un-caveated on JS-built pages | medium | JS-render caveat added (`428a0d4`) |

> Note: the two finders that phrased this as "vacuity warn *suppresses* the
> no-op warn" were **refuted** at the gate level — the no-op warn is gated on
> `verdict === "pass"`, so a vacuity-warn doesn't suppress it. But that check is
> census-order based; posthog's section genuinely reorders (order changes) while
> the `fullPage` screenshot misses it, which order-comparison can't catch. The
> visual-delta guard closes that gap directly.

### Confirmed, documented, deferred (golden-contracted or larger change)

- **No guarantee/money-back detector** (`extract.ts` extractTrustSignals) and
  **social-count regex misses word-quantified counts** ("thousands of teams") —
  the heading-proof recall gap (lever #1). The guarantee pattern already exists
  lab-side (`src/adaptive-lab/detectors.generated.ts`, `inventory.ts` tracks
  `trust.guarantees`); it just needs porting into the redesign/prospect
  extractor. Golden-contracted → CI-validated follow-up.
- **Static freeze strips `srcset`/`sizes` and pins geometry only on the browser
  path**, and **webfonts/@import sheets are left as absolute URLs** the demo
  render then blocks — the root of blank/near-blank frozen copies (lever #2).
  Customer-facing harm is already contained by the visual-delta guard (blank
  before/after is suppressed, not shown).
- **`aboveFold` "not visible on first screen" rests on a document-order proxy**
  that nav/logo anchors inflate — hedged in copy ("correct it in Settings"), but
  a measured fold position would be more honest.
- **"Section X of Y" counts nav/footer headings on pages with no `<main>`**
  (`mainRegion` falls back to the whole document — e.g. Basecamp's h1-in-header),
  so the position count can be off. Scoping the count to real content sections
  would tighten it.

### Confirmed, test-harness only (not customer-facing)

- Per-site **timeout is bucketed as `crashed`** rather than a distinct `timeout`
  state; **rerun keeps stale non-crashed rows** verbatim (worked around here by
  clearing `results.json` before each generation). Neither fired in this run.

### Refuted (verified as non-issues)

- "Vacuity warn suppresses the no-op warn" (×2) — refuted mechanism (above).
- "stdout/stderr pipe-drain deadlock" — Bun.spawn lacks Node's 64KB pipe-fill
  blocking semantics.
- "`proc.kill()` orphans the Chromium grandchild" — Playwright's default SIGTERM
  handler closes the browser gracefully.
